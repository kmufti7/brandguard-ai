# Brief: USER_PERSONAS.md

Produce three concrete personas for BrandGuard AI users using the Jobs-to-be-Done framework. Each persona must have name, role, company-size context (e.g., Series B SaaS, 80 employees), and the job statement (when [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]). Include day-in-the-life with NAMED TOOLS (Marketo, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Iterable, etc.) and TIME DURATIONS. Include a 'what they fear about AI doing this' section per persona that names a specific fear (loss of control, brand drift, legal exposure) and explains how BrandGuard addresses or fails to address it.
