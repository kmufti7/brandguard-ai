# DJ-entry rubric (base + extensions)

Inherits the four base dimensions from base.rubric.md. Pass floor 7. Word floor 250.

## Dimension 5: Falsifiable Thesis
- 9-10: The "Why This Choice" section makes a claim that could be tested or refuted by a specific observation.
- 7-8: Thesis is testable but phrased loosely.
- 4-6: Thesis is an opinion stated as principle.
- 0-3: No falsifiable claim.

## Dimension 6: Interview Framing Buzzword-Free
- 9-10: The 30-second interview framing avoids the banned phrase list and reads as a direct answer.
- 7-8: One mild generic phrase; rest is direct.
- 4-6: Several generic phrases; reads as marketing.
- 0-3: Buzzword-heavy; would not survive a technical interviewer.

## Dimension 7: 250-Word Floor
- 9-10: Entry is 250 words or more; word count is meaningful, not padded.
- 7-8: 250+ words with minor padding.
- 4-6: 200-249 words.
- 0-3: Below 200 words; entry is too thin to be a decision record.
