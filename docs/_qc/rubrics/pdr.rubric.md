# PDR rubric (base + extensions)

Inherits the four base dimensions (Specificity, Evidence Trail, Honest Scope, Voice) from base.rubric.md. Pass floor 7.

## Dimension 5: Counter-Thesis Honesty
- 9-10: A "What This Forecloses" or equivalent section names the strongest objection a reviewer could fairly raise, in the reviewer's voice.
- 7-8: Counter-thesis present; phrased gently.
- 4-6: Mentions a trade-off but doesn't engage with it.
- 0-3: No counter-thesis; reads as decision advocacy.

## Dimension 6: Trade-Off Visibility
- 9-10: Every option considered is named, with what each one signals and why it was rejected.
- 7-8: Most alternatives named; one or two rejected without reasoning.
- 4-6: Alternatives mentioned in passing.
- 0-3: No alternatives; decision presented as inevitable.

## Dimension 7: Foreclosed Paths Section Present
- 9-10: Explicit "Foreclosed Paths" or "What This Forecloses" section listing capabilities or futures this decision rules out.
- 7-8: Section present; one or two foreclosures stated.
- 4-6: Foreclosure implied not stated.
- 0-3: No foreclosure discussion.
