# USER_PERSONAS rubric (base + extensions)

Inherits the four base dimensions (Specificity, Evidence Trail, Honest Scope, Voice) from base.rubric.md. Pass floor 7.

## Dimension 5: Persona Concreteness

- 9-10: Every persona has a name, role title, company-size context (e.g., "Series B SaaS, 80 employees"), and a job-to-be-done statement.
- 7-8: Personas have names and roles; company context is implied not stated for one persona.
- 4-6: Generic personas ("a marketing manager").
- 0-3: No personas; reads as audience description.

## Dimension 6: Day-in-the-Life Specificity

- 9-10: Each persona's day includes named tools (e.g., Marketo, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Iterable) and time durations (e.g., "spends 2 hours on Tuesday reviewing copy").
- 7-8: Named tools present; time durations approximate.
- 4-6: Vague workflows ("they review copy").
- 0-3: No day-in-the-life detail.

## Dimension 7: AI Fears Section Present

- 9-10: Each persona has an explicit section about what they fear when AI does this job (loss of control, brand drift, legal exposure, etc.). Fears are persona-specific, not generic.
- 7-8: AI fears section present for most personas.
- 4-6: Fears mentioned in passing.
- 0-3: No discussion of AI risks from the persona's POV.
