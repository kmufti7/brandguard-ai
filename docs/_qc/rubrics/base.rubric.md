# Base rubric (applies to every doc)

Score each dimension 0..10. Pass floor: 7 on every dimension. Below 7 in any
dimension triggers a revision cycle.

## Dimension 1: Specificity

- 9-10: Every claim is concrete (named tools, numbers, durations, file paths).
- 7-8: Most claims are concrete; one or two pass with reasonable generality.
- 4-6: Several claims are abstract or vague when specifics were available.
- 0-3: The doc reads as generic; "could apply to any product" feel.

## Dimension 2: Evidence Trail

- 9-10: Every factual claim is cited or sourced; readers can verify.
- 7-8: Most claims have evidence; one or two are author-asserted without harm.
- 4-6: Multiple claims lack citation when sources exist in the repo.
- 0-3: Author asserts; no evidence trail.

## Dimension 3: Honest Scope

- 9-10: Doc states what it covers AND what it does not. No overreach.
- 7-8: Scope is mostly clear; one or two areas where coverage is implied but not stated.
- 4-6: Scope is fuzzy; doc implies broader coverage than it has.
- 0-3: Doc reads as a complete solution when it is not.

## Dimension 4: Voice

- 9-10: Reads like Strand Wireless brand voice (plain, direct, numbers-first, no filler).
- 7-8: On-brand with minor lapses.
- 4-6: Tone drifts; some sentences read corporate or generic.
- 0-3: Off-brand throughout; reads as marketing copy from elsewhere.
