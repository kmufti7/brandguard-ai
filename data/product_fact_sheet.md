## Product Line Overview

The Strand Mobile Plans family is a five-SKU lineup covering single-line entry, single-line performance, multi-line family, premium unlimited, and small-business use. All SKUs run on the same nationwide 5G network. SKUs differ on data allowance, hotspot, streaming quality, international data, and customer-support tier.

Every SKU's pricing, allowance, and restriction below is the source of truth for RAG-grounded copy generation. Each entry has a stable field ID for citation by the RAG Copy Generation Agent (`[fact_sheet:<SKU>:<field>]`).

## SKU 1: Strand Essentials

- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:price]`** $25.00 per line per month, autopay required for this price (otherwise $30.00).
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:data]`** 5 GB high-speed data per month. After 5 GB, speeds drop to 256 Kbps for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:network]`** 5G and 4G LTE access. Not eligible for 5G Ultra Wideband.
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:hotspot]`** Mobile hotspot not included.
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:streaming]`** Streaming quality capped at 480p (DVD-equivalent).
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:international]`** Talk and text in Mexico and Canada included; international data not included on this SKU.
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:restrictions]`** Single-line only. Autopay enrollment required for the $25.00 rate. Taxes and fees additional.
- **`[fact_sheet:essentials:eligibility]`** New and existing customers with light data needs; typically a single user under 5 GB per month.

## SKU 2: Strand Pro

- **`[fact_sheet:pro:price]`** $45.00 per line per month with autopay (otherwise $50.00).
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:data]`** 50 GB high-speed data per month. After 50 GB, speeds drop to 1 Mbps for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:network]`** 5G, 5G Ultra Wideband, and 4G LTE access.
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:hotspot]`** 10 GB of mobile hotspot included per month at high-speed; reverts to 1 Mbps after the 10 GB cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:streaming]`** Streaming quality capped at 1080p HD.
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:international]`** Talk and text in Mexico and Canada included. 2 GB of high-speed international data per month in 210+ countries; 256 Kbps after the 2 GB cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:restrictions]`** Single-line or multi-line. Autopay required for the $45.00 rate. Taxes and fees additional.
- **`[fact_sheet:pro:eligibility]`** Single users who want a high data ceiling without paying for true unlimited.

## SKU 3: Strand Family

- **`[fact_sheet:family:price]`** $35.00 per line per month with autopay; minimum two lines, maximum six lines. Example billed totals: 2 lines = $70.00, 4 lines = $140.00, 6 lines = $210.00.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:data]`** 30 GB high-speed data per line per month, pooled across the household up to a 120 GB shared ceiling. After the shared ceiling, all lines drop to 512 Kbps for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:network]`** 5G, 5G Ultra Wideband, and 4G LTE access on all lines.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:hotspot]`** 5 GB of mobile hotspot included per line per month at high-speed; reverts to 1 Mbps after the cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:streaming]`** Streaming quality capped at 1080p HD on all lines.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:international]`** Talk and text in Mexico and Canada on all lines. 1 GB high-speed international data per line per month in 210+ countries; 256 Kbps after the cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:restrictions]`** Minimum two lines. All lines on a single account; no line-level billing splits. Autopay required across the account. Taxes and fees additional.
- **`[fact_sheet:family:eligibility]`** Households of two to six people sharing a single account.

## SKU 4: Strand Unlimited+

- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:price]`** $70.00 per line per month with autopay (otherwise $75.00).
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:data]`** Unlimited data with a 100 GB high-speed soft cap per line per month. After 100 GB, speeds may be reduced to 5 Mbps if the network is congested in the user's local area; speeds return to full when congestion clears. The plan name "Unlimited+" must be paired with the 100 GB soft-cap and 5 Mbps post-cap disclosure in any marketing copy that uses the word "unlimited" (per brand voice §4 rule 2).
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:network]`** 5G, 5G Ultra Wideband, and 4G LTE; priority access during local congestion events.
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:hotspot]`** 30 GB of mobile hotspot included per month at high-speed; reverts to 5 Mbps after the cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:streaming]`** Streaming quality up to 4K UHD where the source supports it.
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:international]`** Talk and text in Mexico and Canada included. 5 GB of high-speed international data per month in 210+ countries; 512 Kbps after the cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:restrictions]`** Single-line or multi-line. Autopay required. The 5 Mbps post-cap speed is contingent on local congestion; off-peak performance may be unaffected. Taxes and fees additional.
- **`[fact_sheet:unlimited_plus:eligibility]`** Single users or households that consistently exceed 50 GB per month and value premium streaming or hotspot.

## SKU 5: Strand Business

- **`[fact_sheet:business:price]`** $55.00 per line per month with autopay; minimum two lines, maximum twenty lines per business account.
- **`[fact_sheet:business:data]`** 50 GB high-speed data per line per month, with a 500 GB shared business ceiling. After the shared ceiling, all lines drop to 1 Mbps for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- **`[fact_sheet:business:network]`** 5G, 5G Ultra Wideband, and 4G LTE on all lines; priority access for business traffic during local congestion.
- **`[fact_sheet:business:hotspot]`** 20 GB of mobile hotspot included per line per month at high-speed; reverts to 1 Mbps after the cap.
- **`[fact_sheet:business:streaming]`** Streaming quality capped at 1080p HD on all lines.
- **`[fact_sheet:business:international]`** Talk and text in Mexico and Canada on all lines. 3 GB high-speed international data per line per month in 210+ countries; 512 Kbps after the cap. Business support routes to a dedicated team in business hours (8am-8pm local).
- **`[fact_sheet:business:restrictions]`** Business EIN required at signup. Minimum two lines. Autopay required across the account. Taxes and fees additional.
- **`[fact_sheet:business:eligibility]`** Small businesses with two to twenty employees who need pooled data, business-priority access, and dedicated support.

## Cross-SKU Notes

- All five SKUs run on the same physical network. SKU differences are policy (priority, allowance, hotspot cap, streaming quality), not coverage.
- The word "unlimited" appears only on SKU 4 (Unlimited+). SKUs 1, 2, 3, and 5 are explicitly capped and must not be marketed with the word "unlimited."
- Autopay is a hard requirement for the published rate on all five SKUs. Marketing copy that quotes the published rate must say "with autopay" in the same sentence (per brand voice §4 rule 6).
- All prices exclude taxes and fees. Marketing copy that quotes a price must not imply the quoted price is the total monthly bill.
